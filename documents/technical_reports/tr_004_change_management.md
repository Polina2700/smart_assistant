# Technical Report: Change Management in ERP Projects
**Author:** Sarah Chen
**Date:** 2022-08-15
**Version:** 1.0

## Overview
ERP projects fail not because of technology but because of people. This report documents NovaTech's approach to change management based on 8 completed projects.

|---------|------------------------|-------------------------|-------------------|
| Balkans Steel | Low | 45% | High |

**Correlation is clear:** More change management investment = better adoption = fewer post go-live issues.

## NovaTech Change Management Framework

### 1. Stakeholder Analysis (Week 1)
Map all stakeholders:
- **Champions:** Enthusiastic supporters — recruit as internal ambassadors
- **Neutrals:** Undecided — focus of change management effort
- **Resistors:** Against change — understand concerns, involve in design

### 2. Communication Plan
- Project sponsor message at kickoff (from C-level)
- Monthly newsletter to all affected users
- FAQ document updated weekly during UAT
- Go-live countdown communications (2 weeks, 1 week, 1 day before)

### 3. Training Approach
NovaTech standard: **Role-based training, not module-based.**
- Don't train accountants on "SAP FI module"
- Train them on "How to process your month-end close in new system"

Training formats used:
- Live workshops (preferred for complex processes)
- Video recordings (for simple transactions, reference)
- Quick reference cards (1 page per key transaction)
- Sandbox environment available 4 weeks before go-live

### 4. Super User Program
For every project with >50 users:
- Identify 1 super user per department
- Train super users 2 weeks before end users
- Super users become first line of support at go-live
- Reduce NovaTech hypercare cost by ~40%

## Lessons Learned

### Balkans Steel (2020) — Poor adoption
- Change management budget cut early in project ("we'll handle it internally")
- No super user program
- Training was 2-day classroom session, 1 week before go-live
- Result: 45% adoption, multiple process breakdowns, NovaTech called back 3 months post go-live at extra cost

### AutoParts (2022) — Excellent adoption  
- Client CEO personally championed the project
- Super user program with 8 champions across departments
- Training started 6 weeks before go-live
- Sandbox available from week 8 of project
- Result: 95% adoption, minimal post go-live issues

---

## Budget Guidance
Change management should be 10-15% of total project budget.
Common mistake: Cutting change management to save money → costs more in post go-live support.

| Project Size | Recommended CM Budget |
|-------------|----------------------|
| <€100,000 | €8,000 - €12,000 |
| €100,000 - €200,000 | €15,000 - €25,000 |
| >€200,000 | €25,000 - €40,000 |

---

## Remote/Hybrid Projects (post-COVID lessons)
Since 2020, most training is hybrid. Key adjustments:
- Shorter sessions (max 2 hours online vs 6 hours classroom)
- More frequent touchpoints
- Recording all sessions for those who miss
- Digital quick reference cards instead of paper
- Microsoft Teams channels per project for ongoing Q&A
