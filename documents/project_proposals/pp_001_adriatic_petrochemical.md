## Executive Summary

NovaTech Consulting proposes a comprehensive SAP S/4HANA implementation for Adriatic Petrochemical Ltd. covering Financial Accounting (FI), Controlling (CO), Materials Management (MM), and Production Planning (PP) modules. The project will be delivered in two phases over 10 months, at a total investment of €280,000.

## Proposed Solution

### Phase 1: Financial Foundation (Months 1-6)
**Modules:** FI (Financial Accounting), CO (Controlling)
**Key Deliverables:**
- Chart of accounts setup aligned with Croatian GAAP
- Cost center hierarchy (3 levels)
- Profit center accounting
- Month-end close process automation
- Integration with existing banking systems (SEPA)
- Management reporting (10 standard + 5 custom reports)

**Go-Live Target:** September 2023

### Phase 2: Operations (Months 7-10)
**Modules:** MM (Materials Management), PP (Production Planning)
**Key Deliverables:**
- Material master migration (estimated 4,200 materials)
- Vendor master migration (estimated 340 vendors)
- Purchase-to-Pay process
- Production order management
- Inventory management (3 warehouses)
- MRP (Material Requirements Planning)

**Go-Live Target:** January 2024
