# Project Proposal — ERP Assessment & Roadmap
**Prepared for:** Balkans Steel d.o.o. (Follow-up engagement)
**Prepared by:** NovaTech Consulting
**Date:** 2023-09-01
**Valid until:** 2023-10-01

---

## Background
NovaTech completed SAP ECC full suite implementation for Balkans Steel in 2020. Client now wants to assess migration to S/4HANA and upgrade options.

## Proposed Engagement: S/4HANA Readiness Assessment

### Scope
- Technical assessment of current ECC system
- Custom object inventory (ABAP programs, reports, enhancements)
- Business process review — identify simplification opportunities
- S/4HANA conversion options analysis (Brownfield, Bluefield, Greenfield)
- Total Cost of Ownership comparison
- Roadmap recommendation

### Duration: 6 weeks
### Investment: €28,000

### Team
- Mark Horvat: Lead (familiar with Balkans Steel from original project)
- Riku Tanaka: Technical assessment (ABAP inventory, custom objects)

---

## Why This Matters
SAP has announced end of mainstream maintenance for ECC in 2027 (extended to 2030 with additional cost). Balkans Steel must migrate to S/4HANA by 2030 at latest.

Starting assessment now allows:
- 2024: Assessment complete, decision made
- 2025-2026: Migration project
- Buffer year before deadline

---

## Preliminary Observations (from original project knowledge)
Balkans Steel had significant customizations during ECC implementation:
- 3 custom reports (dispatch, production planning, management summary)
- 2 ABAP enhancements (goods movement, costing)
- Integration with legacy steel production system

These customizations will require careful analysis in S/4HANA context.
