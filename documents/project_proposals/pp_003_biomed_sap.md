## Executive Summary

NovaTech Consulting proposes SAP implementation for BioMed Solutions GmbH to replace the current Oracle E-Business Suite system. Based on client's RFP dated 2022-08-30, we understand client is running Oracle EBS R12 and wishes to migrate to "the latest SAP platform."

**Proposed Solution:** SAP S/4HANA 2022 (cloud edition)
**Total Investment:** €195,000
**Duration:** 8 months
**Go-Live Target:** July 2023

---

## Scope

### Modules Included
- FI (Financial Accounting) — German GAAP compliance
- CO (Controlling)
- MM (Materials Management)
- QM (Quality Management) — critical for medical device compliance
- Regulatory reporting module for MDR (Medical Device Regulation)

### Key Requirements Addressed
1. Full audit trail for medical device traceability (MDR requirement)
2. Batch management with expiry date tracking
3. Integration with LIMS (Laboratory Information Management System)
4. Multi-currency (EUR, USD, GBP)
5. Intercompany processes (BioMed has subsidiary in Austria)
