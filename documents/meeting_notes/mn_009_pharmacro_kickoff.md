Budget: €175,000 | Duration: 8 months | Target go-live: February 2021

## Key Discussion Points

### Regulatory Context
PharmaCro manufactures pharmaceutical raw materials. Subject to:
- EU GMP (Good Manufacturing Practice)
- FDA 21 CFR Part 11 (electronic records/signatures)
- Croatian HALMED requirements

This means SAP must be **validated** — additional documentation, testing protocols, IQ/OQ/PQ validation required. Tomasz has done this before (previous pharma project in 2019).

### QM Module Requirements
- Inspection lots for every goods receipt
- Certificate of analysis (CoA) management
- Batch management with full traceability
- Stability study tracking
- Out-of-specification (OOS) handling workflow

### Key Risk: FDA 21 CFR Part 11
Electronic signatures in SAP for QM approval workflows must comply with FDA requirements. This requires specific SAP configuration and documentation. Mark confirmed NovaTech has done this once before. Will consult with SAP partner network for best practice.
