# Meeting Notes — Balkans Steel Project Post-Mortem
**Date:** 2020-04-15
**Attendees:** Lena Bauer, Mark Horvat, Sarah Chen (internal only)
**Classification:** Internal — Confidential

## Project Summary
- Original budget: €380,000
- Final cost: €425,000 (+€45,000, +11.8%)
- Timeline: 14 months planned → 18 months actual (+4 months)
- Scope: SAP ECC Full Suite (FI, CO, MM, PP, SD, WM)

## Root Causes of Overrun

### 1. Scope Creep (€30,000 impact)
Three formal change requests approved during project:
- CR1: Additional custom report for steel production tracking (€8,000)
- CR2: Integration with legacy rolling mill system — not in original scope (€15,000)
- CR3: Multi-currency extension for export sales (€7,000)

Mark: "We should have identified CR2 and CR3 in blueprinting. We didn't ask the right questions about export business."

### 2. Change Management Failure (€10,000 impact — post go-live support)
- 45% user adoption at go-live
- NovaTech had to provide 3 months additional support at reduced rate (goodwill)
- Root cause: no super user program, training was one-day classroom session

### 3. Resource Issues (timeline impact)
- Mark was 70% on this project, 30% on another — not sufficient for complexity
- Junior consultant turnover (one junior left NovaTech during project)

## What We Learned

1. **Full suite projects require dedicated PM** — Sarah was 50/50 split, not enough
2. **Steel/manufacturing is complex** — more discovery needed for industry-specific processes
3. **Change management is not optional** — cutting it costs more post go-live
4. **Get client CEO commitment in writing** — Balkans Steel CEO was not engaged after kickoff

## Client Relationship
Client satisfaction: 3.2/5.0 — lowest in NovaTech history.
Despite poor score, client came back in 2023 for S/4HANA assessment — relationship salvaged.

## Policy Changes Made After This Project
- Minimum PM allocation: 80% on projects >€200,000
- Mandatory super user program for >50 users
- Change management budget locked (cannot be cut)
