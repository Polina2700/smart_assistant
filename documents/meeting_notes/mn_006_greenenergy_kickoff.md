## Project Overview
GreenEnergy is a renewable energy company (solar + wind) with 45 employees. First ERP implementation — currently using Excel and QuickBooks. Moving to SAP S/4HANA Public Cloud (FI/CO only).

## Key Discussion Points

### Why SAP Public Cloud
- Small IT team (1 person — Tina)
- No need for heavy customization
- Standard energy sector chart of accounts fits their needs
- Fast go-live required — investors want financial reporting by Q4 2023

### Scope
- FI: General Ledger, Accounts Payable, Accounts Receivable, Asset Accounting
- CO: Cost Center Accounting, Internal Orders (for project tracking per wind farm)
- Budget: €89,000 total
- Duration: 4 months

1. **Lisa Park first PM role** — Anna Wolff acting as senior advisor

GreenEnergy receives EU renewable energy subsidies. Accounting treatment is complex — deferred income recognition over asset lifetime. Anna checked: SAP public cloud DOES support this via standard grant accounting functionality. No customization needed. ✓
