## Lessons from Adriatic Petrochemical (Sarah Chen)

### What Worked
- Weekly steering committee kept client engaged
- Using standard SAP Best Practices accelerators saved ~3 weeks
- Anna Wolff's FI workshop approach — hands-on from day 1, not slides

### What Didn't Work
- Mark's split allocation was a real problem. Two weeks in June he was effectively 0% on Petrochemical due to BioMed crisis. Sarah had to cover technical decisions she wasn't qualified for.
- **Recommendation: No consultant should be split below 70% on any single project**

## Lessons from BioMed Solutions (Mark Horvat)

### Root Cause of Problems
The system version mistake (ECC vs S/4HANA) happened because we relied on client's RFP without independent verification. Client IT said "latest SAP" — we assumed S/4HANA.

### Process Change Agreed
From Q3 2023: mandatory technical discovery call with client IT within first week. Checklist to be created by Riku.

### On Budget Overrun
Mark: "We should have flagged the cost center issue as a risk in blueprinting. It was a known complexity — we just didn't price it in."
Lena: "This is why we now require risk-adjusted estimates."

## Lessons from MediaGroup Project (Riku Tanaka) — 2021 project, referenced for comparison
- NAV to BC migration: biggest lesson was that customizations in NAV rarely migrate cleanly
- MediaGroup had 23 custom reports — only 4 could be reused in BC
- Always audit customizations before quoting migration effort
